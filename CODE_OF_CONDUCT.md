# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a welcoming place for people who contribute code, documentation, testing, bug reports, translations, gameplay feedback, and deployment knowledge.

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully and focus discussion on improving the project.

## Expected behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

- giving actionable, technically specific feedback;
- assuming good intent while still addressing mistakes clearly;
- respecting different experience levels and backgrounds;
- keeping issue and pull-request discussions on topic;
- protecting users' privacy and credentials.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory or demeaning comments, deliberate disruption, publishing another person's private information, or knowingly posting credentials or sensitive security details in public threads.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, remove, or reject comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate these expectations. Repeated or severe violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-controlled community spaces.
